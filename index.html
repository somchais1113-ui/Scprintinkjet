Sticker materials

Explore sticker materials

Explore six printed materials, from paper labels and adhesive films to non-adhesive banner vinyl. Choose based on the actual application.

Paper stickers

White paper with a soft matte finish for dry packaging, boxes and bags.

Kraft paper stickers

Natural brown paper with visible fibres. A warm finish for dry goods and kraft packaging.

White PP stickers

Smooth, opaque polypropylene film for labels that need a clean white base.

Clear PP stickers

Transparent polypropylene film that lets the packaging colour and texture show through.

PVC vinyl stickers

Opaque, flexible vinyl often used for decals and surfaces exposed to moisture.

Banner vinyl

PVC-coated flexible banner stock for signs and large-format displays; normally non-adhesive.
