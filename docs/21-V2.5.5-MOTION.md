# V2.5.5 — Motion Design System

## Design rationale

Premium, restrained movement; all interactions remain functional without animations. The verified two-row Hero grid (`10-hero-layout-fix.css`) is unchanged.

## Effects

| Component | Effect | Duration / trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Hero text & CTA | Staggered rise/fade | First load, 0.8–1 s |
| Hero image | Subtle zoom-out over crossfade | 6 s per slide |
| Carousel dots | Delicate active-state highlight | At slide switch |
| Main sections & cards | IntersectionObserver entrance | Once on scroll |
| Card images | Minor scale-up | Hover-capable devices |
| Buttons | Small lift + soft shadow | Hover / press |
| Small overlap images | Slow floating motion | Hover-capable devices |
| Reading progress | Thin 2px top line | Scrolling |

## Animation source files

- `src/styles/11-motion.css`: all motion keyframes, transitions, hover design and reduced-motion rules.
- `src/scripts/motion.js`: progressive enhancement, scroll reveals, reading progress and dynamic card re-observation.
- `src/scripts/main.js`: carousel auto-play is paused in background or reduced-motion preference.

## Editing motion

- Change `--pc-motion-time` and `--pc-motion-ease` in `11-motion.css` for reveal timing.
- Change `6000` (milliseconds) in `src/scripts/main.js` for carousel interval. Keep the image animation duration synchronized.
- Change `pc-enter-up` or `pc-photo-breathe` animation timings in `11-motion.css` for Hero visual pacing.
- Remove the two CSS/JS asset references from `index.html` to disable the extra effects; the existing V2.5.4 site remains functional.

## Accessibility and performance

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables extra effects and carousel auto-rotation.
- Animation primarily uses `opacity` and `transform` instead of changing layout geometry.
- Scroll reveal gracefully falls back to visible content if IntersectionObserver is unavailable.
- Content remains in the document and can be read with assistive technology even before intersection animations complete.
- The website is still a prototype with demo and connected modes; animations do not change Backend status.

## Release procedure

Deploy the **complete** project, not `index.html` alone. CSS/JS references are cache-versioned to `v=2.5.5`. Check Desktop and Mobile in TH/EN.
