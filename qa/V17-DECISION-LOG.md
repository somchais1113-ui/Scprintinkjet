# V1.6–V1.7 Decision Log
- Preserve V1.5.1 homepage **byte-for-byte**. All new visual work confined to Smart Quote and Workbench.
- Customer workflow: no account, no artwork upload, optional Drive link, one-page price summary, copy to clipboard or LINE draft.
- A reviewed rate card is still an estimate; onsite special die cutting, artwork proof, shipping and VAT treatment must be checked before confirmation.
- Cost master is a **local prototype**, not a production CRM, ERP, permissioned cost vault or secure public admin.
- Public price card must be exported explicitly after verified review; no auto-publish and no private cost fields in public package.
- Preserve legacy calculator for backwards compatibility; do not silently replace pricing logic elsewhere.
