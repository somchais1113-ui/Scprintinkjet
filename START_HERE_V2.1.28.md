# SCWork V2.1.28 — release notes

This release is based **only on the V2.1.27 Full Package**. It includes the latest 6-item Material Gallery.

## Scope
- Four portfolio full-bleed image slides with Main Page-like motion and controls. 20 previous work images and filtering remain.
- Shorter price CTA labels (e.g. เช็กราคา), maintained estimate disclaimers; no price tables created.
- Removed visually redundant disclaimers and switched client logo animation to icon pause.
- Larger yet regular-weight type in service comparison cards and more compact footer links.
- Six material entry links: white paper, kraft, white PP, clear PP, adhesive PVC, banner vinyl. Vinyl is NON-adhesive banner stock and must be manually quoted.
- The publicly selectable `pp-silver` item was replaced by `vinyl-banner`; historical/internal source price data was not changed.

## Review before deploying
- All images of people/processes are illustrative mockups or AI imagery, not real SCWork staff or proven client projects.
- Do not use visual samples as certificates for durability or actual stock specifications.
- Publish an approved rate card only through authorized pricing workflows.
- Check real domain, branding, LINE contact configuration and live link behavior after deployment.

For Public Website run `node qa/build-public-v2128.mjs <new_output_folder>` from master root. Do not publish admin tools.
