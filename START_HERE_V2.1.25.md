# SCWork V2.1.25 — Homepage Portfolio Visual Update

Based on V2.1.24. This release changes **only** the three pictures in the homepage Portfolio section, their descriptive alt text, and the disclosure shown in Thai/English. It retains existing approved functionality.

## Visual mapping
1. Product labels: AI illustration of an Asian-looking worker checking printed labels; industrial printer and finished bottles visible.
2. Die-cut stickers: AI illustration of a worker checking a shaped sticker with cut-machine output and finished pieces.
3. Large format: AI illustration of a worker applying storefront graphics, supported by printer and vinyl-roll shots.

**Important:** The workers and production facilities in these three new images are illustrative simulations. They are not photographs of actual SCWork employees, SCWork facilities, or verified customer projects. Replace with approved real photography when available.

## Deployment
Use the Public Website ZIP, not the Full Package. Verify responsive cards at desktop and mobile. When editing assets later, update only `assets/images/portfolio/` or the three image references in the `sc-portfolio` section of `index.html`.
